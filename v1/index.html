Issuer-side EUDI wallet integration for token issuers

Why does every fund need its own copy of your ID?

Nachweis helps token issuers accept EUDI identity evidence and apply their approval to customers' linked crypto wallets, without putting identity documents on chain.

Spoken openingYour ID wallet should work where you invest.

Watch the scene What is real today

The person

Lena finished onboarding with a fund issuer last month. Now she wants to subscribe to its tokenized fund share from her own wallet, and later trade it in a pool.

Today

Each issuer identifies her from scratch: a document scan, a video call or a vendor session, and a copy of her ID in each issuer's file. When she brings her own wallet, the address sits on the issuer's allowlist and every venue that lists the token inherits that list.

With Nachweis

The issuer asks her state ID wallet for exactly three things: given name, family name, over 18. It matches them to its own record, approves, and writes a decision for her address to a registry. The fund token and the pool read that decision. Neither learns her name.

How it works

Input

State ID wallet, SD-JWT PID over OpenID4VP. Issuer trust enforced, status list checked, fail-closed.

Later: mdoc with ZK proof, zkPassport as a labelled lower tier.

Decision

EligibilityDecision
  subject     address, proven by signed nonce
  predicates  uint16 bits, no attribute in clear
  tier        1 state PID · 2 passport ZK
  expiresAt   min(credential expiry, policy ttl)
  policyId    keccak of the published policy
  statusRef   points at the revocation bit

Signed by the issuer's verifier, written to a registry on Sepolia. Names never enter the object.

Outputs

Fund token transfer check reads the registry. Uniswap IAllowlistChecker reads the same entry.

Later, one adapter each: ERC-3643 claim issuer, EAS schema, x402 challenge for APIs.

One revoke in the registry closes every door at once; the consumers share nothing but the schema.

Why now

2027

From 2027, eIDAS 2.0 obliges regulated relying parties to accept the state wallet where they identify customers. Wiki reading of the regulation; the exact article and date per sector are unverified here.

0 of 5,446

Showcase projects across ETHGlobal events that mention the EUDI wallet. Of 302 identity projects, 6 reached a final, each with identity inside a product a person wanted, none as a layer.

0

Live products that accept an external eligibility attestation without a contract. Coinbase and Kraken each have one attester; Uniswap's permissioned factory has no adapter on mainnet.

Read the third fact both ways: nobody plugs us in this year, and the issuer is therefore the only buyer.

Who pays, and what is open

The buyer is a fund issuer or transfer agent that must identify investors and already keeps an allowlist. No operator has been contacted. The after-event gate is a named operator accepting a measurable pilot by 2027-03-31.

Questions to the builder

  1. Matching. Name plus age is not a unique identifier. How does the issuer match the presentation to its investor record? Beat 3 assumes it can.
  2. Binding. The address binding is signed by the Privy key, not by the EUDI wallet. Is that acceptable to say on camera?
  3. Privy. Privy has no Continuity track; eligibility for the reused verifier is unanswered since Sep 1. Ask again, or plan without the prize?
  4. Uniswap. The track is "Stack Contribution" and submissions are audited. Does a checker contract on an unmodified factory count?
  5. Names. The moderator wanted "we hold no data". The scene sends names to the issuer. Which sentence do you say?
